> Announcing his crackdown on crypto, the senator's slides would not convert to PDF, so he held his laptop up to the cameras and read the future of money off a phone photo of his own laptop screen.

A single satirical sentence roasting lawmakers who would regulate AI, crypto or the internet but cannot convert a file to PDF. The angle: a senator announces his crypto crackdown, but his slides will not convert to PDF — so he holds his laptop up to the cameras and reads the future of money off a phone photo of his own laptop screen.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It validates the sentence (one sentence, at most 320 characters), prints it, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` for the grader.
